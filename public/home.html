Security & Privacy

Your security is our priority

Multiple layers of protection keep your account and money safe.

🔐
Password Protection
Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds). Never stored in plain text, never visible to staff.
🔒
Session Security
Sessions stored server-side. Cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax to prevent XSS and CSRF attacks.
📱
OTP Verification
One-time passwords expire in 10 minutes, single-use, bcrypt-hashed. After 3 fails the OTP is locked.
🛡️
Account Lockout
After 5 failed login attempts your account locks for 15 minutes to prevent brute-force attacks.
🔑
Role-Based Access Control
28 granular permissions across 9 roles. System Admins cannot move money. Approval requires a different officer.
🛡️
Bank-Grade Security
Multiple protection layers secure your account and personal information.
